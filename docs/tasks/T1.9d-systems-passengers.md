# T1.9d — ai/, Passenger/Spawn/Trigger/Portal/Goal systems + PassengerView

**Milestone:** M1 · **Depends on:** T1.9b · **In parallel with:** T1.9c · **Size:** L (~1.7K lines of AS3)

## Goal
Passengers appear at stations, walk, wave, board the shuttle, get off at the right station and walk home. Triggers and portals work, level goals are counted.

## Read before starting
- `reference/as3/ru/alientransporter/ai/*` (ConditionList 114, ConditionName 28, ILogic, ISense, Schedule 113, StateName 24) and `ai/passenger/*` (8 files, 534 lines)
- `systems/`: `PassengerSystem` (146), `SpawnSystem` (109), `TriggerSystem` (147), `PortalSystem` (102), `GoalSystem` (173)
- `views/PassengerView` (221), `components/{PassengerMediator,AIBehavior,ActionBehavior,WaitingTimer,SpawnManager,SpawnPoint,KeyPoint,Trigger,Portal,GoalManager,CargoHold}`

## Steps
1. `ai/` — 1:1: `Schedule`/`ConditionList`/`StateName`/`ConditionName` (schedules, conditions), `PassengerLogic`, `PassengerSense` and the schedules `PassengerIdle/Move/MoveToShuttle/MoveToStation/MoveToHome/Action`.
2. Systems — 1:1. Their links with `StationSystem` (T1.9c) are through the original's components and signals.
3. `PassengerView` — 4 colors × 5 types × Idle/Walk/Action; leave the ragdoll parts (`Frag*`) for T2.1 if they are needed only on death (`STUB(T2.1)`).
4. `PortalSystem` — in 2P mode the portal waits for both (`isTwoPlayerMode && numNodes == 2`), as in the original.
5. `GoalSystem` — level goals (`goalKind`, `goalValue`, `targetAliases`) and stars (`LevelPreferences.allStarGoal`).
6. Tests (headless, Level01, fixed seed):
   - a passenger appears within the expected number of ticks (reference);
   - a scripted pilot (input from a prepared script) delivers a passenger: the goal counter grows, the portal opens;
   - `Trigger` fires when the shuttle enters the rectangle (`once` is respected).

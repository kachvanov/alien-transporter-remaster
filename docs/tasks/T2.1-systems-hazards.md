## Steps

2. `SensorSystem` — the sensor logic (rotation, delays, `blinkerAlias`, missile activation). The beam touching the shuttle comes from AntLight (T2.4). Until T2.4 — `STUB(T2.4)` with a temporary geometric "shuttle inside the sector" test, marked as temporary.

4. `MenuSystem` — if it is responsible for in-game menus or buttons (see the code), port it. If it is tied to screens — leave `STUB(T2.6)`.
5. Remove the `STUB(T2.1)` stubs from earlier tasks.
6. Tests (headless):
   - Level08 (rocks): rocks with `actionDelay` fall on the expected tick;
   - Level11 (barrels): a missile hit or a strike on an explosive barrel destroys the barrel and damages a nearby shuttle (damage reference);
   - Level13: the missile launches after the sensor is activated (temporarily via a STUB test);
   - the magnet pulls a coin within `magnetRadius`.
